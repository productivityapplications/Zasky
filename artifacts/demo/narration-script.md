# Zasky local protocol/transcript demo

Synthetic data only. Model: openai/gpt-4o-mini via OpenRouter.

## 01 — ZASKY

This is Zasky. A personal planning agent that lives in a private chat room. It doesn't give me discipline. It makes coming back cheap.

## 02 — Year → month → week → day

The plan has a spine: year goals, monthly commitments, an approved week pool, and one day. Zasky never invents tasks. I name the work; the application does the arithmetic.

## 03 — One must. Two should.

On Friday, the plan has one must and two should tasks. The app shows the latest wake time from my preparation buffer. The day is small enough to start.

## 04 — “Back. A two-hour call ran over.”

Then the day breaks. I say: back, a two-hour call ran over, it's 4pm. I don't rebuild the list from scratch. Zasky already has the approved week pool.

## 05 — Keep what fits. Move the rest.

Zasky reads the approved week pool and proposes a recovery. It keeps what still fits, moves the rest within the same week, preserves fixed events, and reports that the week holds with 270 minutes of work against a 1,066 minute remaining budget.

## 06 — The owner controls the commit.

Nothing changes until I approve. I send approve, with the proposal code. The deterministic parser commits the plan. The model cannot approve itself.

## 07 — Facts. Arithmetic. A next action.

Now the day is workable again. I ask what's left this week and get a short, evidence-backed answer. The commitments are still mine.

## 08 — What this demo is — and is not

This demo is a local protocol transcript, not a Discord screen capture. It uses synthetic data and the real OpenRouter model. The Switch transport is running separately.
